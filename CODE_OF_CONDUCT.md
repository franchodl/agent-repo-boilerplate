# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a welcoming, constructive place to collaborate.
Treat people with respect, give feedback about work rather than individuals,
and keep discussions relevant and considerate.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and abusive behavior may not
acceptable. Project maintainers may edit or remove content and may limit
participation when needed to keep the community safe.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer
[@franchodl](https://github.com/franchodl). Please do not report sensitive
incidents in a public issue.
